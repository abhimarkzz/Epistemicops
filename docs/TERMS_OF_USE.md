## 1. Nature of the Software

EpistemicOps is an open-source, local-first research prototype and demonstration platform designed for Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) incident investigation and procedural runbook memory.

The software is provided for **educational, experimental, evaluation, and demonstration purposes only**. It runs locally on the user's infrastructure and does not operate as a managed software-as-a-service (SaaS) or cloud-hosted subscription product.
